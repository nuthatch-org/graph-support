# Who runs what now

**Last checked:** 2026-10-14. This changes monthly; check the latest Graph Office Hours TL;DR on forum.thegraph.com before naming an owner.

**Asked as:** "who do I ask about Studio", "is Edge & Node still running X", "who fixes the upgrade indexer", "is Lodestar down", "where did the stopgap subgraph go".

## The Foundation

Since the August 2026 handover the Foundation owns Subgraph Studio, the Explorer, the upgrade indexer, the gateway and chain deals (GOH TL;DRs: forum topics 7036, 7054, 7062). The Council voted no on the E&N Labs deal. Some E&N people still answer in the channels; that does not change who owns the fix. Studio is heading to being network-powered via DIPS and the upgrade indexer is to be deprecated. BNB and Polygon Studio traffic moved to the network on 8 October 2026 and Studio support ends 31 October 2026 (#45).

People named in the TL;DRs: Pedro (Head of Product), Orion (Head of Protocol & Network); protocol team Tomas, Miguel (ex-E&N), Carlos (ex-GraphOps, network infra), Samuel, Juan.

The routing table by symptom is [reference/escalation.md](../../reference/escalation.md).

## Our own services

Stood down on 13 October 2026 for lack of funding: Lodestar, Kittiwake, Foghorn, the stopgap subgraphs at `subgraphs.nuthatch-indexer.com` (410), tap-aggregator, the Academy (learn-thegraph.com shows a notice), and 67 Graph repos made private, including this one. Making the forks private closed GIP PR 89, indexer PR 1258 and docs PR 1119.

Chief still answers support questions on the side. Nuthatch itself carries on and is not a Graph service.
